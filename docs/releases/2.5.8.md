## 简体中文

### 2.5.8：按发送队列溢出治理 LHDC 卡顿，新增调试日志开关

- 码率拥堵治理器（实验性，默认关闭）现在把系统发送队列溢出作为直接依据。系统蓝牙栈的发送队列满了会整队丢弃，造成约 225 ms 的断音。现在 30 秒内出现 2 次溢出就降一档（1000 → 900 → 500）；出现过溢出的时段不再算作恢复证据，避免链路仍拥塞时过早回到 1000。
- 以前治理器主要看 BQR 的重传和无响应。手机上另一条连接抢占蓝牙空口时，这两项可以一直显示正常，同时却在频繁断音，治理器既不降档，又会提前恢复。PJZ110 + Enco X3 实测（192 kHz 音质优先，有空口争用，每组 3 分钟）：断音从 46 次降到 14 次，900 → 500 约 3.5 秒完成，降到 500 后约 2.5 分钟没有断音。
- 诊断页新增「调试日志」开关，默认关闭。关闭时，BQR 等高频遥测不再写入 LSPosed 日志，只保留警告、错误和关键状态，减少日志写入与耗电。「开始记录问题」期间会自动输出完整日志，反馈包不受影响。实测关闭时 LHDC 播放 3 分钟不再产生 BQR 日志。
- 每次队列溢出记录一条 `lhdc.link.queue_flush`，并始终写入 LSPosed 日志；反馈包摘要记录调试日志开关状态。

### 升级说明与验证边界

- 空口争用持续存在时，治理器会长期停在 500，用码率换取不断音。本次排查确认的一个常见争用源是电脑上的「手机连接（Phone Link）」：它约每 30 秒通过蓝牙反复连接手机。在电脑上关闭它的蓝牙连接后，PJZ110 + Enco X3 可以稳定播放 1000 kbps。
- 启动保护期内需要 3 次溢出才降档，这一规则只经过单元测试，尚未实机验证；其余行为已在 PJZ110 / ColorOS 17 上实机验证。
- 升级后，旧版默认输出的高频日志不再写入 LSPosed 日志。排查问题请使用「开始记录问题」，或临时打开「调试日志」，排查结束后再关闭。
- 安装或更新模块后需要重启蓝牙进程或重启手机，新的治理逻辑才会生效。

## English

### 2.5.8: Govern LHDC stutter by TX queue overflow and add a debug-log switch

- The bitrate congestion governor (experimental, off by default) now acts directly on TX queue overflow. When the system Bluetooth stack's TX queue fills, it drops the whole queue, causing a ~225 ms audible gap. Two overflows within 30 s now step down one rung (1000 → 900 → 500). Periods that contained an overflow no longer count as recovery evidence, so the governor does not return to 1000 while the link is still congested.
- Previously the governor relied mainly on BQR retransmission and no-response rates. When another connection on the phone competes for Bluetooth airtime, those rates can stay normal while audio keeps dropping, so the governor neither stepped down nor stayed down. On PJZ110 + Enco X3 (192 kHz Quality Priority, airtime contention present, 3 min per run), audible gaps fell from 46 to 14, 900 → 500 completed in about 3.5 s, and 500 then played for about 2.5 min with no gaps.
- Adds a "Debug log" switch to the diagnostics page, off by default. While it is off, high-frequency telemetry such as BQR samples is no longer written to the LSPosed log; only warnings, errors and key status are kept, reducing log writes and battery use. "Start recording issue" still enables full output automatically, so feedback packages are unaffected. In device testing, 3 min of LHDC playback produced no BQR log lines with the switch off.
- Each queue overflow is recorded as `lhdc.link.queue_flush` and always written to the LSPosed log; the feedback summary records the debug-log switch state.

### Upgrade notes and validation limits

- Under sustained airtime contention the governor stays at 500, trading bitrate for uninterrupted playback. One common source confirmed in this investigation is Phone Link on a PC, which reconnects to the phone over Bluetooth about every 30 s. After turning off its Bluetooth connection on the PC, PJZ110 + Enco X3 played 1000 kbps stably.
- The rule that requires 3 overflows inside the stream start guard is covered by unit tests only and has not been verified on a device; the rest of the behavior was verified on PJZ110 / ColorOS 17.
- After upgrading, the high-frequency logs that older versions wrote by default no longer reach the LSPosed log. To investigate an issue, use "Start recording issue" or temporarily enable "Debug log", and turn it off afterwards.
- After installing or updating the module, restart the Bluetooth process or the phone for the new governor logic to take effect.
