## 简体中文

### 2.5.5：修复 ColorOS 17 LHDC 编码器 Hook 被系统覆盖

- 修复一加 15 / ColorOS 17 反馈中的 `streaming=false`、`actualBitrateKbps=0`、`queue=-1`：系统在每次加载 LHDC 编码器时都会重写 `A2DP_VendorLoadEncoderLhdcV5` 的 dlsym 函数表，模块此前只安装一次 Hook，表被覆盖后就再也看不到编码器。
- Native 侧现在会检查已记录的 encode / free 槽位：Hook 仍然有效时快速返回，被系统覆盖时重新写回 wrapper；Java 侧在每次 LHDC 配置确认和 BQR 样本到达时做自愈检查，避免一次安装失败或后续覆盖导致整场播放失明。
- 诊断页和反馈包新增 `governorInstalled`、`governorInstallResult`、`bqrFallbackCapKbps`、`leakyFallbackCapKbps`，便于确认码率保护是否真正下发。

### 升级与边界

- 安装后重新加载官方「无线耳机」及蓝牙作用域，建议切到「音质优先」后重新连接耳机并播放至少 30 秒再抓反馈。
- 本次确认的是 Hook 生命周期缺陷：系统重写函数表后模块无法读取编码器状态，保护目标也就无法生效。实际听感是否完全恢复仍取决于耳机、采样率与无线链路环境，需要升级后复测。

## English

### 2.5.5: Restore the LHDC encoder hook after ColorOS 17 replaces it

- Fixes the `streaming=false`, `actualBitrateKbps=0`, and `queue=-1` state in the OnePlus 15 / ColorOS 17 feedback: the stack repopulates the dlsym table in `A2DP_VendorLoadEncoderLhdcV5` on every encoder load. The module installed its hook only once, so the table replacement made the governor blind for the rest of playback.
- The native side now checks the recorded encode/free slots. It returns quickly while the wrappers are still active and reapplies them after the vendor reload overwrites the table. The Java side performs the same self-healing check on every confirmed LHDC config and BQR sample, so an early failed install no longer leaves the session blind.
- Adds `governorInstalled`, `governorInstallResult`, `bqrFallbackCapKbps`, and `leakyFallbackCapKbps` to diagnostics and feedback so the next capture can show whether protection was actually applied.

### Upgrade notes and limits

- Reload the official Wireless Headphones app and Bluetooth scopes after installing. Select Sound Quality Priority, reconnect the headset, and play for at least 30 seconds before capturing feedback.
- This release confirms the hook-lifecycle defect: once the vendor reloaded its function table, the module could not observe the encoder and its protection target could not take effect. Whether playback is fully recovered still depends on the headset, sample rate, and radio environment and needs verification after upgrading.
