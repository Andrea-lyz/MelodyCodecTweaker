## 简体中文

### 2.5.6：优化音质优先发送节奏并修复耳机面板兼容

- 治理器关闭、使用 LHDC V5 音质优先且系统原周期为 20 ms 时，将编码发送节奏调整为 10 ms；保持所选码率、采样率和 PCM 总量不变。PJZ110 / ColorOS 17 实际起流记录确认周期为 10 ms、码率保持 1000 kbps。
- 更稳健地定位「无线耳机」中的弹窗、菜单和选项，减少 ColorOS / OPlus 宿主混淆变化造成控制项回退；LE Audio 确认弹窗继续使用原生 COUI 底部样式。
- 手动选择 LHDC 或 LDAC「音质优先」前显示无线环境提示。点「确定」才会切换，点「取消」或返回不执行切换。
- 升级后，已有「音质优先」耳机记忆的用户首次进入对应耳机页面时会看到一次风险提醒；提醒不改变已恢复的播放质量或采样率。

### 升级说明与验证边界

- 10 ms 发送节奏旨在减小每次编码发送的瞬时批次，不会降低固定 1000 kbps，也不能增加无线链路容量；高负载 Wi-Fi 或信号干扰下仍可能卡顿，改善幅度需按实际设备和环境试听确认。
- 新增提示与 COUI 弹窗已通过本地单元测试及 APK 构建检查；弹窗在各 ColorOS / OPlus 版本上的实际外观和确定、取消交互仍需安装后实机复核。

## English

### 2.5.6: Smooth Quality Priority pacing and improve headset panel compatibility

- With the governor disabled, LHDC V5 Quality Priority selected, and the OEM interval set to 20 ms, encoder pacing is changed to 10 ms. The selected bitrate, sample rate, and total PCM amount are preserved. A real PJZ110 / ColorOS 17 stream confirmed a 10 ms interval while the bitrate remained 1000 kbps.
- Improves runtime resolution of dialogs, menus, and options in Wireless Headphones to reduce regressions caused by ColorOS / OPlus host obfuscation changes. The LE Audio confirmation dialog keeps its native COUI bottom-sheet style.
- Shows a wireless-environment warning before a manual switch to LHDC or LDAC Quality Priority. The switch runs only after confirmation; Cancel or Back leaves the current selection unchanged.
- After upgrading, users with a saved Quality Priority choice see a one-time reminder when they first open that headset's page. The reminder does not change the restored playback quality or sample rate.

### Upgrade notes and validation limits

- The 10 ms pacing aims to reduce the instantaneous batch size per encoder period. It does not lower a fixed 1000 kbps bitrate or add wireless link capacity; stutter may still occur during heavy Wi-Fi transfers or interference, and the listening improvement depends on the device and environment.
- The new notices and COUI dialogs passed local unit-test and APK-build checks. Their rendered appearance and confirm/cancel interaction still need on-device verification across ColorOS / OPlus versions.
